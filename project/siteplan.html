Site Plan: Foteate with Aura – Take Photos with Good Vibes

Site Name

Foteate with Aura

This name captures the idea of taking photos that reflect positive energy and genuine emotions. “Foteate” comes from the Spanish slang for “take a picture,” while “Aura” represents the good vibes and light around every person or moment.

Optional domain: foteatewithaura.com

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to showcase photography sessions that focus on authentic emotions and natural light. Visitors can explore past projects, learn about the available photo packages, and book their own session online. The goal is to share creativity, joy, and real human connection through photography.

Scenarios (Audience Questions)

  • How can I book a photo session that fits my style and budget?
  • Where can I see examples of previous sessions and photography styles?
  • What kind of atmosphere or “aura” do you create during your photo shoots?

Color Schema

The color palette reflects natural light, calm tones, and creativity:

  • Aura Green – #4E9A7D (used for headings and accents)
  • Soft Beige – #F7F3EF (background color)
  • Charcoal Gray – #333333 (body text)
  • White – #FFFFFF (contrast areas)

Typography

Headings: Bebas Neue – modern and confident for main titles.

Body Text: Open Sans – friendly and readable for paragraphs.
