1. Project Subject
My website will be for a photography business that offers professional photo services such as portraits, 
weddings, events, and product photography.

2. Description of Planned Content
The site will include:

- A homepage that introduces the business and shows some featured photos.
- A services page with details and pricing packages.
- A contact page with a form so people can reach out and book a session.

3. Reason
I chose this subject because photography is both a creative passion and a valuable service. 
I want to have a place where people can see my work, learn about my services, and easily get 
in touch with me if they’re interested.
